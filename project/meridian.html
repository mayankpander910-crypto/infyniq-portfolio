Meridian

A considered e-commerce experience for a mechanical watch atelier — collections, product pages, journal, events and a private-viewing booking flow.

Challenge

What needed to change.

Meridian needed a storefront that felt as precise as the watches it sells: calm editorial storytelling, clear product discovery across four collections, and commerce details (warranty, returns, servicing) that build trust without shouting.

Approach

How we built it.

We designed a restrained, type-led interface with a serif display voice, structured collection and product templates, an archive-sale module with countdown, an events calendar and a long-form journal. The build is fully responsive with fast image loading and accessible navigation.

Deliverables

What we shipped.

  • Storefront & collection architecture
  • Product detail templates with variants
  • Archive sale & countdown module
  • Events / private viewing booking
  • Journal & newsletter capture
  • Responsive, performance-minded build
