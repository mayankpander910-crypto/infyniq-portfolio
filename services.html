FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Have questions about our services, process or pricing? We’ve answered the most common ones to help you get started with ease.

What services do you offer?

Web development, mobile app development, UI/UX design, digital marketing, SEO and social media management — plus dedicated resources and ongoing maintenance.

How does your process work?

We begin with a discovery call to understand your goals, then move through design, development, testing and launch with regular check-ins. After launch we offer support and growth services.

What are your pricing models?

Fixed-scope project pricing for defined builds, and monthly retainers for ongoing development, marketing or dedicated resources. We’ll recommend the best fit after understanding your needs.

How long does a typical project take?

A business website typically takes 2–4 weeks; e-commerce and web apps 4–10 weeks; mobile apps depend on scope. We share a clear timeline before we start.
